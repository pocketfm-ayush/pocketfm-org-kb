# Skill: Role — Scout

**When:** Fast read-only reconnaissance (where is X? what files touch Y?).
**Not for:** Deep research briefs (use `role-researcher`) or edits.

## Process

1. Search quickly (grep/glob/pack index).
2. Return file:line hits + one-line why relevant.
3. Flag uncertainty; do not fill gaps with invention.

`SCOUT: DONE` with paths list. Escalate to researcher if load-bearing.
