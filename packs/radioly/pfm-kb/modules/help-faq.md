# Help FAQ

Aliases: FAQ, help center, frequently asked questions

## What It Is

The **Help** list: categories, articles, search, related, top
searches. Not History FAQ (wallet). Not subscription FAQ.

## Actors

Listener. Disabled articles stay hidden. Internal tools can see
those.

## Journey

1. App opens Help → categories with at least one live article.
2. Open a category or search.
3. Article. Optional “was this helpful” + email.

## User-Visible Invariants

- Empty Help is no categories, not a failed wallet History.
- Disabled copy must not show.

## Client Behavior

Needs network. Feedback needs an email.

## Security / PII / Compliance

Feedback email and comments are support PII. Do not invent
retention.

## Gaps

Live category list and whether Help is every market / vintage are
not recorded here.
