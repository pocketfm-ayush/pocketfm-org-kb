# Terms and conditions

Aliases: T&Cs, terms, legal copy

## What It Is

Legal copy for a **named screen / campaign**. Not Help FAQ. Not a
receipt.

## Actors

Listener on the screen that asked. Editors publish by tag.

## Journey

1. App asks for that tag’s terms.
2. Missing tag → not found, not a crashed paywall.

## User-Visible Invariants

- Do not reuse History or Help copy as this screen’s terms.
- One tag, one document.

## Client Behavior

Needs network. Empty is “no terms,” not a payment failure.

## Gaps

Which tags the app asks for are not recorded here.
